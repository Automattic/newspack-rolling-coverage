This section is for developers at newsrooms and agencies who extend Rolling Coverage or connect it to other tools. Editors and publishers don't need it to run a coverage.

Each page starts with what the thing is for, then shows how to use it. If one of them isn't something the site needs, skip it.

<Cards>
<Card title="REST API" href="/docs/developers/rest-api/" description="Read coverages and entries, and add entries from another app or service." />
<Card title="Hooks" href="/docs/developers/hooks/" description="Actions and filters that react to what the plugin does or change it." />
<Card title="Constants" href="/docs/developers/constants/" description="Settings a site can fix in wp-config.php, such as how often readers' pages check for new entries." />
<Card title="Data" href="/docs/developers/data/" description="How coverages and entries are stored, for queries, migrations, and reports." />
</Cards>

The plugin repository's [contributing guide](https://github.com/Automattic/newspack-rolling-coverage/blob/trunk/.github/CONTRIBUTING.md) is the full reference. It also covers the plugin's architecture and every feature that these pages leave out.
