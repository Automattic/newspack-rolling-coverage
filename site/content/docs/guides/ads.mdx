---
title: Ads between entries
description: Show ads between entries in a feed, set how often, and turn ads off for one coverage.
---

By the end of this page, a feed will show an ad every few entries, and you will know how to keep ads off a sensitive coverage.

This needs the Newspack Ads plugin. Turning on the placement needs an Administrator account. Setting up a feed needs an Editor or Administrator account.

## Turn on the ad placement

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Newspack Ads

In the dashboard, open the Newspack Ads placements.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enable the Rolling Coverage placement

Find **Rolling Coverage: Entry**, enable it, and pick an ad unit.

<Screenshot label="Newspack Ads placements, Rolling Coverage: Entry placement enabled with an ad unit" />

</Step>
</Steps>

## Set ads on a feed

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Ads panel

In the editor, select the Rolling Coverage block and open the **Ads** panel. It appears only when Newspack Ads is active.

</Step>
<Step>

### Turn advertising on

Set **Advertising** to **Enabled**. This isn't available when **Show** is set to **Latest**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose how often

Set **Ads interval**. The default is 4, which shows an ad after every fourth entry. Pinned entries count.

<Screenshot label="Block settings, Ads panel with Advertising and Ads interval" />

</Step>
</Steps>

The panel tells you when the placement isn't enabled, or when ads are off for the coverage.

## How many ads show

Up to three ads appear among the entries that load with the page, and the older ones readers load after them. With an interval of 4, that is after entries 4, 8, and 12, and none further down.

New entries that arrive while the page is open get an ad after every fourth one, with no limit.

Feeds that show only the latest entries have no ads.

## Turn ads off for one coverage

For a sensitive story, such as an emergency, keep ads off.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Edit the coverage

In **Rolling Coverage > All Coverages**, open the coverage's row actions and select **Edit**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Disable advertising

Set **Advertising** to **Disabled**, then select **Save**. Feeds for this coverage show no ads.

<Screenshot label="Edit Coverage panel, Advertising set to Disabled" />

</Step>
</Steps>
