By the end of this page, you will have turned an entry into a full post, and the feed will point readers to it.

Creating a breakout post needs an Editor or Administrator account.

## Create a breakout post

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the entry's actions

In **Rolling Coverage > All Coverages**, open the coverage's entries. Open the row actions of the entry that deserves a full story.

<Screenshot label="An entry's row actions menu, with Create Breakout Post picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/full-stories-1.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Select Create Breakout Post

Select **Create Breakout Post**. The site creates a draft post with the entry's title, content, categories, tags, and featured image.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the full story

Open the draft and develop it as a normal post. Publish it when it is ready.

</Step>
</Steps>

The entry and the post stay linked. An entry that already has a breakout post doesn't offer **Create Breakout Post** again.

If the post is in the trash, the entry's actions offer **Restore Breakout Post** and **Permanently Delete Breakout Post**.

## What readers see

Until the post is published, the entry shows as usual. Once it is published, the feed shows the entry as a Full story card. The card holds:

- the **Full story** label;
- the post's featured image, when it has one;
- the post's title;
- the start of its summary;
- the entry's own date, author, and Share button.

The whole card links to the post. A pinned entry works the same way.

Changes to the published post, such as a new title, summary, or featured image, reach open pages at their next check for updates. If the post is unpublished or deleted, the entry shows its own title and content again.

Layouts differ in how the card looks. Margin and Split show no card, but still show the label, title, image, and summary. Ticker, which is compact, shows the post's title in the entry's place.

A Coverage Status block on the breakout post shows the coverage the post came from. See the [Coverage Status block](/docs/blocks/coverage-status/).

## Change the Full story label

To change the label for every story, go to **Rolling Coverage > All Coverages** and select **Settings**. On the **Labels** tab, set **Full story label**. See [Settings](/docs/guides/settings/).

To give one story its own label, such as "Analysis", open the breakout post in the editor. In the **Rolling Coverage** panel of the post's settings, set **Full story label**. This panel shows only on posts made with **Create Breakout Post**.

Either label can be up to 40 characters.

## If the card doesn't show

An edited built-in layout or a detached copy saved before the Full story card existed, or a custom layout without one, shows the entry as a normal entry. For a built-in layout, [restore it](/docs/guides/edit-a-layout/#restore-a-built-in-layout) to get the card. Restoring changes every page that uses the layout, so read that section first. For a detached copy, pick the layout again with **Change Layout**, then check the block's settings. For a custom layout, duplicate a current built-in layout and rebuild from there.
