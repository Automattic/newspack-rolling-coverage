---
title: Archive entries
description: Mark an entry as out of date without removing it from the feed.
---

By the end of this page, you will know how to archive an entry that has been overtaken by news, and how to bring it back.

Archiving needs an Editor or Administrator account. Only published entries can be archived.

## Archive an entry

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the entry's actions

In **Rolling Coverage > All Coverages**, open the coverage's entries. Open the row actions of the entry to archive.

<Screenshot label="Entry row actions menu with Archive highlighted" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Select Archive

Select **Archive**. The entry stays on the page, marked as out of date.

</Step>
</Steps>

To bring it back, select **Unarchive** in the same menu.

You can archive or unarchive several entries at once. Select their rows, then choose the action in the bar at the bottom of the list.

## What readers see

A notice appears above the entry: "This entry is now out of date compared to newer entries, but is preserved as it originally appeared." If the site renamed entries under **Settings**, the notice uses that name.

A long archived entry collapses to a summary. Readers can expand it.

## What to know

- An archived entry is locked. It offers no publishing, moving to draft, **Reassign**, **Pin**, or **Create Breakout Post** until it is unarchived. Its content can still be edited.
- Archiving isn't available once the coverage has ended.
- An archived entry whose full story is published shows the post's card, without the out-of-date notice.
