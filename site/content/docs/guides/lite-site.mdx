When the site uses the Lite Site plugin, a page with a liveblog also has a text-only copy. This page says what readers see there.

This needs a Lite Site release after 0.1.0. With version 0.1.0 or older, the text-only page shows the entries as plain text, as they were when the page was saved. New entries don't arrive, and older ones don't load.

## What readers see

- The feed keeps updating. New entries arrive, and older ones load, as the block is set.
- Every layout looks the same: a plain, text-only style where each entry opens with its time, then its title and content.
- A thin rule separates entries.
- Pinned entries and Full story entries show as cards.
- Entries arrive and leave without fades, and new ones show no "New" label.
- Load more and Check for updates buttons look like Lite Site's own.
- The liveblog name, the status badge, and the link to the liveblog page still show.

## What doesn't show

Ads, Share buttons, and the Follow Coverage button don't appear on text-only pages. The status badge doesn't show when the liveblog was last updated.

## Full stories

An entry whose full story is published shows the **Full story** label, the post's title, and its excerpt. The title links to the post's own text-only page, or to the post when it has none. See [Full stories](/docs/guides/full-stories/).
