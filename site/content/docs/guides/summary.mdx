title: Summary

By the end of this page, you will know how to add a summary to a liveblog, keep it up to date, and remove it.

A summary is a short entry, usually a heading such as "What we know" over a few bullet points, that readers see in a box at the very top of the liveblog. You update it in the liveblog, like any entry, so you never have to edit the page during a story, and pages already open show each change.

Adding a summary needs an Editor or Administrator account. Each liveblog has at most one.

To choose between a summary, pinned entries, and key takeaways, see [Summary, pinned entries, or key takeaways?](/docs/guides/summary-pinned-or-takeaways/).

## Add a summary

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the liveblog's entries

In **Rolling Coverage > All Liveblogs**, open the liveblog's entries. At the top right, open the **More actions** menu and select **Add Summary**. A liveblog with no entries yet shows an **Add Summary** button in the middle of the page instead, beside **Add Entry**.

<Screenshot label="A liveblog's entries list, with the More actions menu open and Add Summary picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/summary-1.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the summary

Type a heading in the title, such as "What we know". Below it, write the summary. A bulleted list works well: type `-` and a space to start one.

<Screenshot label="The Add Summary editor, with the title What we know and four bullet points written" src="/screenshots/guides/summary-2.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish it

Select **Publish**. The summary appears at the top of the list with a "Summary" label, and the box appears at the top of the liveblog.

<Screenshot label="The entries list with the summary first, its Summary label picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/summary-3.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

To keep it as a draft until it's ready, select **Save Draft** instead. Readers see the box only once the summary is published.

## Update the summary

Open the summary's row actions and select **Quick Edit**, or **Edit** to open it in the full editor. Change the text, then select **Save**.

Pages that are already open show the new version at their next check for updates, without a reload.

## Remove the summary

Open the summary's row actions and select **Trash**. The box leaves the liveblog, including on pages that are already open, and **Add Summary** comes back.

To bring the summary back, restore it from the **Trashed** filter. A liveblog can't have two summaries, so trash the current one first.

## What readers see

<Screenshot label="The Kettle River flood page, with the What we know box at the top of the liveblog" src="/screenshots/guides/summary-4.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

The box sits at the top of the liveblog, above pinned entries. It shows the summary's title, its text, and "Updated" with the time it was last edited, in your site's time format. It has a light gray background, like a pinned entry in Bulletin. "Updated" looks like the times on the entries, and the box's corners match the layout's other boxes.

The summary isn't an update:

- It never shows in the list of entries.
- It doesn't count as a new entry and gets no "New" label.
- It doesn't send a push notification to followers.

## What to know

- Every layout that shows the whole liveblog has the box. Layouts that show only the latest entries leave it out: Ticker, Wire, Digest, Flash, and Alert.
- In a custom layout, the box is the group named "Summary". It is locked in place like the Pinned Entry. Unlock it to move it, for example below the entries.
- The summary can't be pinned, archived, or given a full story.
- With no published summary, the liveblog shows no box.
