By the end of this page, you will know how to pin an entry, how several pinned entries are ordered, and what readers see.

Pinning needs an Editor or Administrator account.

## Pin an entry

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the entry's actions

In **Rolling Coverage > All Coverages**, open the coverage's entries. Open the row actions of the entry to pin.

<Screenshot label="Entry row actions menu with Pin highlighted" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Select Pin

Select **Pin**. The message "Entry pinned." appears, and the list marks the entry as pinned.

</Step>
</Steps>

To undo it, open the same menu and select **Unpin**. The message "Entry unpinned." appears.

Use a pinned entry for something every reader needs, such as a summary of where things stand or a road closure.

## How pinned entries are ordered

A pinned entry stays at the top of the feed, above newer entries, whatever its date.

With more than one pinned, the one edited most recently comes first. A summary that is kept up to date therefore stays on top.

Pinning counts as an edit, so a newly pinned entry goes to the top. So do archiving, reassigning, and updating an entry's full story.

An unpinned entry returns to its place by date.

## What readers see

The entry carries a "Pinned" label, a pin icon, or both, depending on the layout. In most layouts it is set apart with the accent color.

Some layouts keep the pinned entry in view while the reader scrolls. Split shows every pinned entry in a column beside the feed. When nothing is pinned, the feed takes the full width.

Pages that are already open show the change at their next check for updates.

## What to know

- Feeds set to show only the latest entries ignore pinning. That covers Ticker, Wire, Digest, Flash, and Alert.
- A pinned entry whose full story is published shows as a Full story card. See [Full stories](/docs/guides/full-stories/).
- Once a coverage has ended, its entries can't be pinned or unpinned until the coverage is set back to **Live**.
