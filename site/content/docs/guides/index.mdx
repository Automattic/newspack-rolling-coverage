---
title: Guides
description: How to get the most out of Rolling Coverage once a coverage is running.
---

The guides go beyond the first coverage. They are being written now; this page lists what they will cover.

- **Layouts**: the 13 built-in layouts and when to use each.
- **Edit and detach a layout**: change a layout for every page that uses it, or for one page only.
- **Make a custom layout**: design a layout with the site's own blocks.
- **Slack**: connect a workspace and a channel, and choose how messages become entries.
- **Pinned entries**: how pinned entries are ordered and shown.
- **Full stories**: turn a major entry into its own post with **Create Breakout Post**.
- **Archive an entry**: mark an entry as out of date without removing it.
- **End a coverage**: what readers see when a story is over.
- **Placements**: every page that shows a coverage, and which one is the main page.
- **Settings**: rename statuses and labels for the whole site.
- **AI key takeaways**: a short summary above the feed as the story grows.
- **Ads**: show ads between entries.
- **Push notifications**: let readers follow a coverage.
