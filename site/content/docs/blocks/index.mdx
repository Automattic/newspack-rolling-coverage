---
title: Blocks
description: The blocks that come with Rolling Coverage, and what each one does.
---

Rolling Coverage adds six blocks to the editor. Find them in the **Newspack** category of the block inserter, or under **Rolling Coverage** on sites without Newspack.

Share and Breakout Post Link are parts of layouts. They don't appear in the inserter, so you don't add them on their own.

<Cards>
	<Card title="Rolling Coverage" href="/docs/blocks/rolling-coverage/" description="The live feed. Shows a coverage's entries in a layout and adds new ones as they're published." />
	<Card title="Coverage Status" href="/docs/blocks/coverage-status/" description="A badge that shows whether a coverage is Live, Paused, or Ended." />
	<Card title="Follow Coverage" href="/docs/blocks/follow-coverage/" description="A button that lets readers follow a coverage and get notifications." />
	<Card title="Update Timer" href="/docs/blocks/update-timer/" description="Tells readers when the feed next checks for new entries." />
	<Card title="Check for Updates" href="/docs/blocks/check-for-updates/" description="A button readers press to load new entries. Goes inside a layout." />
	<Card title="Load More" href="/docs/blocks/load-more/" description="The button that loads older entries. Goes inside a layout." />
</Cards>
