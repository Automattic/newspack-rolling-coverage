---
title: Scoreboard block
description: Show a match's score above the liveblog, and keep it current on open pages.
---

The Scoreboard block shows a liveblog's score: the two team names with a score for each on one row, a dash between the scores, and a short status line beneath, such as "Second half" or "Full time". The status line can show as a badge, in the same styles as the Coverage Status block. When you change the score, readers who have the page open see the new score without reloading.

The score belongs to the liveblog, so you set it once, in the liveblog's entries list, and every Scoreboard block for that liveblog shows it.

## Where to use it

- **Above the feed.** Add it to a match's page or post, above the Rolling Coverage block.
- **Inside a Rolling Coverage layout.** Add it to the layout's header. It shows that block's liveblog, and the **Liveblog** panel is hidden. It can't go inside an entry or the pinned entry.

## Add a score to the liveblog

A liveblog has no score until you add one, so the block stays hidden on the site until then. When you add a score, readers who have the page open see the scoreboard appear without reloading. Editors and administrators can add a score.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the liveblog's entries

Go to **Rolling Coverage** > **All Liveblogs** and select the liveblog.

</Step>
<Step>

### Name the teams

At the top of the page, open the **More actions** menu and select **Add Score**. Type the **First team** and **Second team** names, then select **Add Score**. The first team shows on the left.

<Screenshot label="The Add Score window, with the First team and Second team fields filled in" src="/screenshots/blocks/scoreboard-1.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Update the score

The score row appears above the entries. Use the + and − buttons beside each team, which save straight away, or type a number and press Enter.

Type a short status line in **Status**, such as "84'" or "Full time", and press Enter.

In **Badge**, pick the badge the status line shows as, such as **Ended** for "Full time". The choices are the Coverage Status badges, with your site's labels. It saves as soon as you pick it, and only shows on Scoreboard blocks that show the status as a badge. See [Show the status as a badge](#show-the-status-as-a-badge).

<Screenshot label="The liveblog's entries list, with the score row above the entries picked out, Status set to Full time and Badge to Ended" src="/screenshots/blocks/scoreboard-2.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

If a change can't be saved, the score goes back to what it was and a message says why.

To rename the teams, open the menu at the end of the score row and select **Edit Teams**. To take the score off the liveblog, select **Remove Score**. Every Scoreboard block for the liveblog then stops showing.

## Scores in different sports

Each team's score is one whole number, from 0 to 999, and the status line holds up to 40 characters. That covers most sports:

- **Points that come in bigger steps,** such as a three-pointer in basketball or a touchdown in American football: type the new total and press Enter, rather than selecting + several times.
- **The period or the clock:** put it in the status line, such as "Q3 4:12", "Second half, 84'", "OT", or "Penalties".
- **Sets and games,** such as tennis: keep the sets won as the score and the games in the status line, such as "Set 3: 4-2".

To fix a mistake, select − or type the right number. Readers who have the page open see the corrected score without reloading.

Judged sports with aggregate totals such as figure skating, events with more than two competitors such as a race, and scores below zero such as golf to par don't fit two whole numbers. Report those scores in the entries instead. These events usually have an individual or team leaderboard, which means more to readers than a single score: keep it current in a pinned entry or the summary.

## Add the block

<Steps>
<Step>

### Insert the block

Open the page, post, or template. Add a block and search for "Scoreboard".

<Screenshot label="The block inserter with Scoreboard in the search results" src="/screenshots/blocks/scoreboard-3.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the liveblog

Outside a Rolling Coverage block, open the **Liveblog** panel in the block settings. Choose **Automatic** or **Custom**. With **Custom**, pick the liveblog from the list.

<Screenshot label="The block settings, Liveblog panel, with Automatic and Custom picked out" src="/screenshots/blocks/scoreboard-4.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

In the editor, the block shows the liveblog's score. In a template, or when the liveblog has no score yet, it shows a sample score so you can style it.

## Show the status as a badge

On a match page, the status line can replace the Coverage Status badge: "Full time" as an Ended badge rather than "Ended" next to "Full time".

<Steps>
<Step>

### Turn on the badge

Select the Scoreboard block. In the **Settings** panel of the block settings, turn on **Show status as a badge**. The status line shows as a badge in the editor straight away.

<Screenshot label="The block settings, Settings panel, with Show status as a badge turned on and picked out, and the status line shown as an Ended badge" src="/screenshots/blocks/scoreboard-6.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick the badge's style

In the liveblog's score row, pick the style in **Badge**. Change it whenever the status line changes, such as **Live** for "Second half" and **Ended** for "Full time".

</Step>
</Steps>

The badge looks like the Coverage Status block's default badge in the same style, with your site's labels for the choices. Its size, colors and font come from the badge, so the status line's own styles don't change it.

## Settings

### Liveblog panel

The panel appears only outside a Rolling Coverage block.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Automatic** | Shows the liveblog on this page, or a breakout post's liveblog. |
| **Custom** | Always shows the liveblog you pick. |

**Automatic** works on single posts and pages. It uses the first Rolling Coverage block in the page's own content.

### Settings panel

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show status as a badge** | Shows the status line as a badge, in the style picked in the score row's **Badge** menu. Off on new blocks. |

### Styling

The scoreboard is made of a row of five paragraphs (the team names, the scores, and the dash between them) and a status line, so you can style each part on its own. Select a team name, a score, the dash, or the status line to change its size, weight, color, or alignment in the **Styles** tab. Select the Scoreboard block itself to give the whole scoreboard a background, text color, border, padding, or margin.

## What readers see

The score updates on open pages without a reload, the badge's text and style included. With a Rolling Coverage block for the same liveblog on the page, it updates each time that block checks for new entries. Without one, the page checks the score itself every 30 seconds while the liveblog is live or paused.

When the score changes, screen readers hear the new score, such as "Riverbend FC 3, Toledo Beacon 1". A change to the status line or its badge isn't read out. Nothing on the scoreboard moves or flashes.

## When it doesn't appear

The block shows nothing on the site when:

- The liveblog has no score yet. It appears as soon as one is added.
- The liveblog has ended without a score.
- There is no liveblog to show: **Automatic** on a page with no Rolling Coverage block, or on an archive page.
